Privacy Policy

Updated 26 October 2026. Instatools is built so your work stays on your device.

Local design and export

The public maker processes photos, headlines, and exports in your browser. We do not upload your images or design data to Instatools servers when you create or download a post.

No account for the maker

You can use the maker without creating an account. Saved designs stay in your browser storage on this device.

API apply form

If you request an API key, the apply form sends your name, email, and message to our form backend so we can reply. Do not send passwords or private keys in that form.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It is cookieless, does not identify you across sites, and is used to count page views and see which pages are used. We do not sell analytics data.

Static assets

Pages may load fonts from Google Fonts and, on API docs, scripts from jsDelivr. Demo layout images are served from a public CDN. That does not change how your own photos are processed in the maker.

What we do not do

  • We do not run advertising networks on this site.
  • We do not sell your photos or form submissions.
  • We do not require an account to export a PNG.

Updates

We may update this page. The date at the top is the latest version.
